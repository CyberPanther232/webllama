About Webllama

Your models. Your machine. Your conversation.

Webllama is a quiet interface for working with Ollama models locally. It leaves the intelligence close to the work and the work close to you.

Built for focus.

A deliberately compact workspace for conversations, drafts, questions, and loose threads. No feeds, no distraction, just a capable model ready to help.

How it works

Local inference

Webllama connects to an Ollama server you control.

Private by default

Your conversations stay on the path you configure.

Configurable

Set your endpoint in Settings and make it your own.
